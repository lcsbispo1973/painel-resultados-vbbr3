Notices to Shareholders
Annual General Meeting Resolutions
—
Rio de Janeiro, April 15th, 2021.
Petrobras Distribuidora S.A. (“Company” or “BR”) (B3: BRDT3) informs its shareholders that
the Ordinary General Meeting was held today and its shareholders approved, by majority:

I- Company's management report, Company's management accounts, Company's financial
statements, Independent Auditors opinions and Company's Fiscal Council opinions for the fiscal
year ended on December 31st, 2020;

II- Company capital budget proposal for the fiscal year to end on December 31st, 2021;

III- Result allocation proposal of the fiscal year ended on December 31st, 2020, including the
dividends distribution in the total amount of R$ 2,305,206,951.34 updated to the date of the
AGM (04/15/2021) based on the variation in the SELIC Rate, reaching the amount of R$
2,316,132,992.30, as follows:

(a) interest on equity already declared and paid, and R$ 498,387,512.18 or R$ 0.42780043964
per share, adjusted by the SELIC rate from 12/31/2020 to 01/12/2021.

(b) dividends in the amount of R$ 1,807,079,933.28, with an amount adjusted by the SELIC rate
from 12/31/2020 to 04/15/2021 of R$ 1,817,745,480.12 or R$ 1.56029654946 to be paid in two
installments, being:

(1) the first installment in the amount of R$ 1,100,000,000.00 or R$ 0.94420600858 with more
monetary restatement of R$ 6,492,298.05 or R$ 0.00557278802 to be paid by April 30th, 2021;

(2) the second installment in the amount of R$ 707,079,933.28, or R$ 0.60693556505 with more
monetary restatement of R$ 4,173,248.79 or R $ 0.00358218780 to be paid until December 31st,
2021.
